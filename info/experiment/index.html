and ratios, can you use them to identify

Click Check Work when you have completed each round. Click Reset Round to start the round over.
In round 1 enter both the of the paternal alleles in the horizontal cells and both maternal alleles in the vertical cells. The capital letter A will indicate dominant body color alleles and the lower case letter a will indicate recessive. Be sure to include all combinations of heterozygous dominant, heterozygous recessive, and homozygous for both parents.
To complete each Punnett square in round 2 remember that each offspring’s genotype includes one paternal allele and one maternal allele. Combine alleles contributed by the parents in each row with each column to indicate the genotypes of all four possible offspring.
In round 3 the genotype ratio (G) gives the number of heterozygous dominant genotypes to homozygous genotypes to heterozygous recessive genotypes, or AA:Aa:aa.The phenotype ratio (P) shows the number of one observable form of the trait (dark body color) to the other form (light body color). It can also be described as the number heterozygous dominant genotypes AND homozygous genotypes to heterozygous recessive genotypes, or (AA+Aa):aa.
In round 4 each of the sets of fly icons illustrates a group of offspring that show a particular phenotype ratio. Compare each set to your completed Punnett squares and determine what parent pairings are likely to have produced that group of offspring. Activate the fly button for an additional hint.
